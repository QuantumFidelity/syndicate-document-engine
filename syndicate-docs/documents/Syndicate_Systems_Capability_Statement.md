## CORE CAPABILITIES

Syndicate Systems, LLC provides coordinated construction, facilities support, and project execution services for government and commercial clients.

The company supports small-scale infrastructure, renovation, repair, maintenance, and facilities-related requirements through subcontractor coordination, vendor sourcing, site support, documentation management, and operational oversight.

Core capabilities include:

- Government contract coordination and execution support
- Construction and facilities project coordination
- Subcontractor sourcing and vendor management
- Site visit scheduling and field operations support
- Multi-vendor coordination and oversight
- Administrative and procurement support services
- Documentation, compliance, and reporting support
- Contract deliverable tracking and operational coordination
- Facilities maintenance and repair coordination
- Infrastructure and specialty trade support coordination
- Simplified acquisition and micro-purchase support
- Rapid-response operational support services

Syndicate Systems utilizes a scalable subcontractor-driven execution model, enabling rapid sourcing and coordination of qualified vendors, labor, and specialized capabilities based on project-specific project requirements.
