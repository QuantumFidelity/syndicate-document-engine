## 1. Purpose and outcomes

Syndicate Systems provides independent systems architecture advisory. We help leaders strengthen system structure so reliability, performance, scalability, and operational clarity improve in measurable ways.

We deliver a clear architectural position, not a list of options.

This engagement will:

- Identify architectural constraints and systemic risk

- Define a target-state direction with decision-ready recommendations
- Reduce ambiguity in priorities, sequencing, ownership, and next steps

You will leave this engagement with a decisive path forward.

## 2. Definitions

The following terms are used consistently:

- **Inspection:** A time-boxed discovery and analysis phase to understand the current system and its operating environment.
- **Architecture & Advisory Report:** The written deliverable summarizing findings, risks, and recommendations.
- **Recommendation:** A proposed change to structure, design patterns, operating model, or technology direction. The client may adopt, adapt, or decline.
- **Execution Roadmap:** A sequenced plan that converts recommendations into implementable workstreams with dependencies and decision points.
- **Stewardship:** Ongoing advisory support to maintain architectural continuity as the system evolves.

## 3. Engagement approach

We follow a structured model that produces actionable guidance with minimal disruption.

We prioritize architectural integrity over short-term convenience.

### At a glance

- **Qualify:** confirm fit and scope
- **Inspect:** establish current-state reality and risks
- **Report:** deliver a decision-ready recommendation set
- **Optional:** roadmap and ongoing stewardship

### 3.1 Qualification (pre-engagement)

Objective: confirm fit, define scope, and align on outcomes.

Activities:

- Short discovery call(s) with key stakeholders
- Confirmation of priorities, constraints, and success criteria
- Agreement on scope, access needs, and schedule

Output:

- Confirmed scope and plan for the inspection phase

### 3.2 Paid inspection (analysis phase)

Objective: establish an accurate current-state picture and surface the highest-leverage risks and constraints.

Inputs we review (as applicable):

- Architecture diagrams and system documentation
- Runbooks, on-call notes, incidents, and postmortems
- SLOs/SLAs and observability signals
- CI/CD flow, environments, and release controls
- Dependency map (internal services and third parties)
- Data flows, ownership, and failure handling

Working sessions:

- Stakeholder interviews (engineering, operations, security, product, leadership)

Notes:

- This is not a compliance audit unless explicitly scoped.
- Findings depend on the access and information provided.

### 3.3 Architecture & Advisory Report (delivery)

Objective: convert findings into clear, prioritized, decision-ready guidance.

The report is written for both executive stakeholders and technical leaders.

### Optional add-ons

- **Execution roadmap:** a prioritized implementation path with dependencies and decision points
- **Stewardship retainer:** continuity as the client executes change and the system evolves

Syndicate Systems operates strictly in an advisory capacity and does not perform implementation.

## 4. Scope of inspection

The inspection focuses on the structures that drive reliability, performance, maintainability, and risk.

We focus on root causes, not symptoms.

### 4.1 Architecture and design

- Service boundaries and coupling
- Pattern fit (what works, what breaks)
- Systemic bottlenecks and single points of failure
- Resilience patterns (timeouts, retries, circuit breakers, bulkheads)

### 4.2 Infrastructure and topology

- Environment structure (dev/stage/prod), parity, and drift risk
- Network boundaries, identity, and access patterns
- Dependency mapping (internal and external)

### 4.3 Operations and delivery

- Release controls, change management, and rollback safety
- Incident response and postmortem rigor
- On-call sustainability and operational load

### 4.4 Data, integrations, and critical paths

- Data ownership and lifecycle
- Integration contracts and failure handling
- Latency and throughput constraints on critical paths

### 4.5 Risk surface

- Reliability risk (availability, durability, disaster recovery)
- Security and privacy considerations (high level, unless scoped deeper)
- Vendor/tool risk (lock-in, fragility, misalignment)

We do not aim to audit every component. We focus on the structural realities that drive system behavior, cost, and operational risk.

## 5. Deliverables

### Deliverable 1: Architecture & Advisory Report

The report typically includes:

- **Executive summary**
    - Key findings and business impact
    - Top risks and constraints
    - Recommended direction and expected outcomes
- **Current-state structural analysis**
    - System overview and dependency map (as available)
    - Primary failure modes and bottlenecks
    - Operational factors affecting reliability and delivery speed
- **Risk and constraint register**
    - Risk/constraint description
    - Likely impact (reliability, cost, security, delivery speed)
    - Evidence and observations
    - Mitigation options
- **Recommendations**
    - Clear recommendation statements
    - Rationale and tradeoffs
    - Dependencies and prerequisite decisions
    - Suggested sequencing (high level)
- **Decision points**
    - Where leadership decisions are required
    - What information is needed to decide

Expect direct, evidence-based conclusions.

### Deliverable 2 (optional): Execution roadmap

If requested, the roadmap typically includes:

- Sequenced initiatives (phases or workstreams)
- Dependencies and gating decisions
- Risk controls during migration and change
- Success criteria and checkpoints

### Deliverable 3 (optional): Stewardship

If requested, stewardship typically includes:

- Periodic advisory sessions (cadence to be agreed)
- Review of proposed changes for architectural coherence
- Ongoing risk monitoring and prioritization support

## 7. Boundaries and exclusions

### What this engagement is

- Independent architecture advisory
- Decision-ready recommendations based on evidence gathered during inspection

### What this engagement is not

- Implementation, engineering delivery, or operational ownership
- A formal compliance audit or certification program
- A penetration test or vulnerability scan

Syndicate Systems maintains strict advisory boundaries:

- **No implementation:** We do not write production code, deploy changes, or modify client systems.
- **No operational ownership:** We do not assume on-call duties or run the system.
- **Vendor independence:** We do not accept referral fees and do not represent vendors.
- **No guarantee of outcomes:** Recommendations reflect the information available during the inspection and are not a warranty.

Unless explicitly included in scope (in writing), the engagement does not include:

- Compliance audits (SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI, HIPAA) or certification preparation
- Penetration testing or vulnerability scanning
- Detailed threat modeling beyond high-level architectural risk identification
- A complete bill of materials for every system component
- Project or program management for execution
