# Letter of Intent / Subcontractor Support Confirmation

**Solicitation:** 140P1426Q0079

**Project:** GTRE Headquarters Chiller Replacement – Grand Teton National Park, Wyoming

**Prime Contractor:** Syndicate Systems, LLC

**Proposed Subcontractor:** Snake River MEP Complete

Syndicate Systems, LLC intends to utilize Snake River MEP Complete as a proposed subcontractor in support of the referenced solicitation.

This letter confirms Snake River MEP Complete's willingness to support Syndicate Systems, LLC in the preparation and performance of the referenced contract, contingent upon award to Syndicate Systems, LLC.

Snake River MEP Complete acknowledges that it has reviewed the solicitation, Statement of Work (SOW), applicable amendments, site visit information, and associated contract documentation. Snake River MEP Complete further confirms its ability to provide the personnel, equipment, materials, transportation, licenses, certifications, technical expertise, and project management necessary to perform the required chiller replacement services.

Specifically, Snake River MEP Complete confirms its ability to support the following requirements:

- Procurement and installation of replacement heat pump/chiller equipment meeting or exceeding the performance requirements outlined in the solicitation.
- Demolition, removal, recovery, transportation, and disposal of existing chiller equipment and associated components.
- Recovery, handling, and disposal of refrigerant, glycol, piping, wiring, and associated materials in accordance with applicable EPA, Federal, State, and local regulations.
- Installation of replacement heat pump/chiller modules and all associated mechanical components required for a complete and operational system.
- Connection of replacement equipment to existing piping, electrical infrastructure, and building systems as required by the Statement of Work.
- Provision and installation of all required electrical wiring, controls, and related components necessary for proper operation.
- Integration of the replacement equipment with the existing Building Automation System (BAS), including coordination with Long Building Technologies as necessary.
- System startup, testing, balancing, commissioning, and performance verification in accordance with manufacturer specifications.
- Provision of qualified personnel possessing all licenses, certifications, and training required to perform the work safely and in compliance with applicable regulations.
- Compliance with all project schedules, milestone requirements, site access procedures, safety requirements, and technical specifications outlined in the solicitation.
- Provision of technical documentation, equipment cut sheets, lead times, commissioning documentation, warranties, and other deliverables required under the contract.

Snake River MEP Complete agrees that any communication with the Government regarding this solicitation shall be coordinated through Syndicate Systems, LLC unless otherwise authorized in writing by Syndicate Systems, LLC.

The parties further acknowledge their intent to negotiate a mutually acceptable subcontract agreement should Syndicate Systems, LLC receive award of the prime contract.

This Letter of Intent is provided solely for proposal support purposes. It does not constitute a binding subcontract, guarantee of award, guarantee of work, or exclusive contractual relationship beyond support of the referenced proposal.

The parties agree to cooperate in good faith during proposal preparation and evaluation activities associated with this solicitation.
