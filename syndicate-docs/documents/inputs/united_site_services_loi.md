# Letter of Intent / Subcontractor Support Confirmation

**Solicitation:** W912WJ26QA098

**Project:** Portable Toilet Services – Birch Hill Dam and Tully Lake Dam, Royalston, Massachusetts

**Prime Contractor:** Syndicate Systems, LLC

**Proposed Subcontractor:** United Site Services

Syndicate Systems, LLC intends to utilize United Site Services as a proposed subcontractor in support of the referenced solicitation. This letter confirms the subcontractor’s willingness to perform the scoped services and to coordinate with the prime contractor for pricing, scheduling, site access, and compliance documentation as required by the contract.

The subcontractor will provide the services described in the solicitation and any applicable attachments, including coordination of labor, equipment, consumables, and reporting necessary for successful performance. Pricing, insurance, and final scope details will be confirmed in the subcontract agreement if the prime contract is awarded.

This letter is provided for proposal support purposes only and does not constitute a binding subcontract or guarantee of award.
