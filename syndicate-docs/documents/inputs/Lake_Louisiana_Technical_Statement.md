# Technical Acceptability / Capability Narrative

## 1. Understanding of Requirements

Syndicate Systems, LLC understands that this requirement is for comprehensive exterior landscape maintenance services at the National Weather Service Weather Forecast Office located at 500 Airport Boulevard, Suite 115, Lake Charles, Louisiana 70607.

The Contractor is responsible for providing all labor, supervision, tools, equipment, transportation, materials, herbicides, fertilizers, and associated services necessary to maintain the facility grounds in a neat, professional, and well-maintained condition throughout the contract period.

Services required under the Statement of Work include, but are not limited to:

- Scheduled mowing of improved, semi-improved, and native grass areas.
- Grass and weed trimming around fixed obstacles, structures, signs, trees, fences, and utility features.
- Edging of sidewalks, curbs, driveways, parking areas, and other paved surfaces.
- Removal and disposal of grass clippings, litter, natural debris, and other accumulated material.
- Fertilizer applications in accordance with the prescribed schedule.
- Weed control using approved herbicides applied by properly licensed personnel.
- Annual pruning and trimming of trees, shrubs, and landscape plantings.
- Fire ant control treatments.
- Annual mulch replacement in landscape beds.
- Reporting of any observed defects, pest infestations, disease conditions, or safety concerns to the Government's designated representative.

Syndicate Systems understands that all work must be performed without disruption to normal Government operations and during the approved service hours of 7:00 AM through 4:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

## 2. Technical Approach

Syndicate Systems will perform this requirement through its proposed landscaping subcontractor, Natchez Lawn & Landscape, LLC dba U.S. Lawns, a commercial landscaping company with extensive experience providing recurring grounds maintenance services throughout Louisiana.

Services will be performed according to the schedule established within the Statement of Work.

### Mowing Operations

The grounds will be maintained in accordance with the required seasonal mowing schedule. Turf areas will be mowed, trimmed, edged, and cleaned during each scheduled visit. Grass clippings will be mulched where appropriate or removed when visible accumulations occur.

Following mowing operations, paved surfaces, curbs, sidewalks, parking areas, and entrances will be blown clean and left free of debris.

### Fertilization

Fertilizer applications will be performed during the required months of February, May, August, and October. Products will be applied according to manufacturer specifications and industry best practices to promote healthy turf and landscape appearance.

### Weed Control

Weed control treatments will be performed during March and June by properly licensed personnel utilizing approved herbicides. Applications will be conducted in accordance with federal, state, and local regulations and product labeling requirements.

### Tree and Shrub Maintenance

Tree pruning and landscape trimming will be performed annually during February. Pruning activities will focus on maintaining plant health, appearance, safety, and clearance requirements while removing dead, damaged, or diseased growth.

### Fire Ant Control

Fire ant control treatments will be incorporated into routine maintenance operations to maintain safe and attractive grounds conditions.

### Mulch Replacement

Landscape bed mulch replacement will be performed annually during the spring season in accordance with the Statement of Work.

## 5. Environmental Compliance

Syndicate Systems understands the requirement to utilize environmentally responsible products whenever practical.

All herbicides, pesticides, fertilizers, and treatment products utilized under this contract will comply with applicable federal, state, and local regulations. Safety Data Sheets (SDS) and product information will be provided to the Government as required by the Statement of Work.

Personnel applying herbicides or pesticides will possess the appropriate state licensing and certifications required for application activities. All pesticide and herbicide applications will be performed by licensed personnel utilizing products approved for use within the State of Louisiana.

## 6. Quality Control

Syndicate Systems will implement a quality control process designed to ensure all services are completed in accordance with contract requirements.

Quality control measures include:

- Supervisor inspection following service visits.
- Verification of mowing, trimming, edging, and cleanup activities.
- Documentation of completed services.
- Immediate correction of identified deficiencies.
- Reporting of landscape concerns, pest infestations, hazards, or property damage to the Government representative.
- Completion of service tickets following each visit.

Monthly invoicing and service documentation will be provided in accordance with contract requirements.
