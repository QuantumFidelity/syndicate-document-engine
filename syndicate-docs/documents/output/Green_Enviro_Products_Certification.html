Solicitation 1305M326Q0221
Project Landscape Services – National Weather Service Weather Forecast Office (WFO), Lake Charles, Louisiana

Certification of Environmental Compliance

Syndicate Systems, LLC hereby certifies that all products, materials, herbicides, pesticides, fertilizers, mulch, and related landscape maintenance supplies utilized in the performance of Solicitation No. 1305M326Q0221 will comply with all applicable federal, state, and local environmental laws, regulations, and requirements.

Syndicate Systems further certifies that:

  1. Environmentally responsible products will be utilized whenever practical and consistent with contract performance requirements.

  2. Herbicides, pesticides, and fertilizers used under this contract will be approved for use within the State of Louisiana and applied in accordance with manufacturer instructions, applicable regulations, and industry best practices.

  3. All pesticide and herbicide applications will be performed by properly licensed and certified personnel in accordance with Louisiana Department of Agriculture and Forestry requirements and all applicable federal regulations.

  4. Safety Data Sheets (SDS) and product information for chemicals, herbicides, pesticides, fertilizers, and other regulated materials utilized under this contract will be maintained and provided to the Government upon request and in accordance with contract requirements.

  5. Products utilized during performance will be selected and applied in a manner intended to protect personnel, property, wildlife, vegetation, and the surrounding environment.

  6. Syndicate Systems, LLC and its proposed subcontractor, Natchez Lawn & Landscape, LLC dba U.S. Lawns, will comply with all environmental provisions contained within the Statement of Work and solicitation documents.

Syndicate Systems, LLC understands that environmental stewardship and regulatory compliance are essential components of successful contract performance and is committed to maintaining safe, professional, and environmentally responsible landscape maintenance practices throughout the period of performance.
