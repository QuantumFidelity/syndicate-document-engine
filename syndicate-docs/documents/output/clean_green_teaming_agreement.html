Letter of Intent / Subcontractor Support Confirmation

Solicitation: [FA480926Q0022]

Project: Fuel/Water Mixture Removal and Disposal Services – Seymour Johnson Air Force Base, North Carolina

Prime Contractor: Syndicate Systems, LLC

Proposed Subcontractor: Clean Green Environmental Services

Syndicate Systems, LLC intends to utilize Clean Green Environmental Services as a proposed subcontractor in support of the referenced solicitation.

This letter confirms Clean Green Environmental Services’ willingness to support Syndicate Systems, LLC in the preparation and performance of the referenced contract, contingent upon award to Syndicate Systems, LLC.

Clean Green Environmental Services acknowledges that it has reviewed the solicitation, Performance Work Statement (PWS), applicable amendments, Questions and Answers, and associated contract documentation. Clean Green Environmental Services further confirms its ability to provide the personnel, equipment, transportation, environmental compliance measures, disposal services, permits, licenses, certifications, and reporting necessary to perform the required fuel/water mixture removal and disposal services.

Specifically, Clean Green Environmental Services confirms its ability to support the following requirements:

  • Fuel/water mixture pumping and removal services.
  • Transportation of collected materials to an approved processing or reclamation facility.
  • Proper disposal, recycling, or recovery of materials in accordance with applicable Federal, State, and local regulations.
  • Compliance with environmental and safety requirements outlined within the solicitation.
  • Provision of personnel possessing current and valid certifications required for performance, including HAZWOPER certification where applicable.
  • Provision of equipment meeting solicitation requirements, including tanker trucks, transfer pumps, hoses, and associated equipment.
  • Submission of documentation required under the contract, including manifests, disposal records, certificates of recycling, permits, and related environmental compliance records.
  • Performance of services within the response timelines established by the Government.

The parties further acknowledge their intent to negotiate a mutually acceptable subcontract agreement should Syndicate Systems, LLC receive award of the prime contract.

This Letter of Intent is provided solely for proposal support purposes. It does not constitute a binding subcontract, guarantee of award, guarantee of work, or exclusive contractual relationship beyond support of the referenced proposal.

The parties agree to cooperate in good faith during proposal preparation and evaluation activities associated with this solicitation.
